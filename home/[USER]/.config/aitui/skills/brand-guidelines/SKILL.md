# Brand Styling

## When to Use

Use this skill when applying brand colors, typography, visual identity, or company design standards to documents, presentations, interfaces, or other artifacts.

## Establish the Brand

Use the user's supplied brand guide, design tokens, approved assets, and existing project styles as the source of truth. If the brand is unspecified and the choice matters, ask which identity to apply. Do not assume a vendor's branding.

## Apply Consistent Styling

- Map primary, secondary, accent, background, and text colors to their intended roles.
- Preserve approved logo proportions, spacing, placement, and variants.
- Use approved heading and body fonts, with appropriate fallbacks when they are unavailable.
- Maintain a clear type hierarchy, consistent spacing, and coherent visual emphasis.
- Use accent colors purposefully without overwhelming the content.
- Match charts, shapes, and illustrations to the selected visual language.

## Verify the Result

Check contrast, readability, alignment, and consistency across pages or screens. Preserve the content's meaning and accessibility. Note any missing fonts or assets and explain substitutions rather than silently replacing approved branding.
